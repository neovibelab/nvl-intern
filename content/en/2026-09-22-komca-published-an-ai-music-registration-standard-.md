# KOMCA Published an AI Music Registration Standard on August 3 and Withdrew It on August 25

**What happened** · The Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and the music industry will hold the first public discussion to establish standards for copyright trusteeship, collection, and distribution of AI-generated music. After the Korea Music Copyright Association (한국음악저작권협회) withdrew its own standards in August, the government called for prior social discussions, highlighting the standard gap among creators, platforms, and policy institutions.

---

On September 22, 2026, at 10 a.m. in the National Assembly Members' Office Building in Seoul, Korea holds its first public hearing on how AI-assisted music should be registered with and administered by copyright collecting societies. The session is co-hosted by the office of lawmaker Kim Jae-won (Kim Jaewon (김재원)) and a coalition of six rights bodies including KOMCA (Korea Music Copyright Association), KOSCAP (함께하는음악저작인협회) and the recording industry association, and the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism plans to follow it with further hearings on royalty distribution rules, according to [AI Times (인공지능신문)](https://www.aitimes.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=42019).

The reason a hearing is needed is a short, awkward episode. KOMCA, which handles performing and mechanical rights for most Korean songwriters, published its own AI music registration standard on August 3 and withdrew it on August 25, as reported by [Asiae](https://view.asiae.co.kr/article/2026082608485585092). The ministry reportedly sent a letter on August 18 urging broader public debate first. That is twenty-two days of life for a national standard. Works registered during that window now go through a cancellation process, and applications merely filed are held until a new policy arrives, per [Yonhap](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260825n30640).

What KOMCA did not withdraw is the judging. It said the retraction does not end the discussion, that generative output without substantial human creative contribution remains ineligible for registration, and that monitoring and review of suspicious filings continue under existing rules, according to [Newsis (뉴시스)](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260825n32866). So the verdicts continue while the measuring marks are blank. What got pulled was not a rate card. It was the method for counting contribution.

Blanks get filled from outside. One stated basis for the original standard was overseas practice and an audit finding, and at this hearing the Korea Copyright Commission (한국저작권위원회) will present a comparison of registration criteria for generative AI works in the United States and elsewhere, as [News1](https://m.news.nate.com/view/20260921n03743) reported. Borrowing the sentence also means borrowing the verification procedure that makes the sentence operable. Deciding whether human contribution was substantial requires comparing the finished track against a work history, and no domestic rule currently specifies that instrument. Whoever owns the measuring tool ends up writing the split formula.

So here is the bet. By the end of 2026, at least one Korean collecting society or music streaming platform will publicly announce an AI-contribution measurement and verification tool, either a procured solution or a technology partnership. The way to check is plain: ministry follow-up hearing materials, KOMCA and KOSCAP member notices, and platform press releases.

Anyone reading the next round of hearing documents should look for two lines before anything about percentages. Which data will be used to confirm contribution, and whether the party doing the confirming is also the party selling the tool.

Any industry writing ESG or provenance rules should notice that the audit instrument, not the threshold, is where control actually sits.

**Prediction** · By end-2026 a Korean CMO or platform will publicly announce its own AI-attribution measurement tool or tech partnership. · within 90 days · check: Check MCST follow-up session materials, CMO notices, and platform press releases for attribution-verification tooling

**Takeaway** · When distribution rules are blank, build the measuring instrument before the rate card - borrow someone else's ruler and they set the marks.
