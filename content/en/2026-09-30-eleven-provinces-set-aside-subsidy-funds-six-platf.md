# Eleven Provinces Set Aside Subsidy Funds, Six Platforms Commit 6 Billion Yuan

**What happened** · China's micro short-form drama industry is expanding a new model combining content with e-commerce. Baidu, YY, and Maishu are collaborating with Taobao to explore content-commerce integration, while cities like Nanjing and Taizhou are promoting IP expansion and commercial-themed productions. China's National Radio and Television Administration is also driving quality improvement through its 'Micro Short-form Drama Original Creation Distribution Plan'.

- **National Radio and Television Administration** · China's ministry-level agency under the State Council that regulates radio, television, and online audio-visual programs. Established in 2018. Chinese government body implementing the quality micro-drama creation and distribution plan.
- **Baidu** · A Chinese internet search company founded in 2000. Chinese company with a micro-drama production unit attached to the Taobao project.
- **Taobao** · A Chinese e-commerce platform under Alibaba Group selling products from individual sellers and small businesses, established in 2003. China's largest online shopping platform testing a format linking drama to checkout.

**Why this one** · As short dramas link directly to Taobao commerce and regulators shift the bar from volume to quality, the unit of negotiation for music IP licensors starts moving from per-episode fees toward sales conversion.

---

A spin-off micro-drama based on the Chinese youth novel *Left Ear* (The Left Ear (左耳)) has entered production, [CNR](https://news.google.com/rss/articles/CBMibkFVX3lxTFBtWXFjX2xDaDYtRk94VXc3TnBIM05PdEw4UzZJZ2dNdUdKNnJMTnhkSmhPR203UktaWVo5VHFQWXdGZEVoRWtIaWpVN24yV1BQb1lpU2U1M3JKd0VfTVpkbGhLVUVLeGxlSUJxck1n?oc=5), the state broadcaster's news site, reported. A Baidu-affiliated production unit and Taobao, China's largest online shopping platform, are attached to it, testing a format that runs the drama and the checkout page together. A micro-drama is a vertical-screen serial of one to three minutes per episode, watched on a phone the way you would watch a short video, except it continues. In the life of an intellectual property, it sits in the same slot a web comic occupies when it is turned into a mobile game. It is the adaptation shelf.

The state has now walked onto that shelf carrying money. The [National Radio and Television Administration](https://www.nrta.gov.cn/art/2026/5/26/art_113_73378.html)'s plan for quality micro-drama creation and distribution was circulated on 23 May 2026 as document 广电办发〔2026〕123号, as Xinhua and the Hunan provincial government each disclosed. Beijing News reported on 15 May 2026, covering a planning meeting held the previous day, that eleven provinces have set aside dedicated subsidy funds and six major streaming platforms have committed at least 6 billion yuan. A mandate framed as raising quality is, in practice, a mandate that moves the production budget per episode.

Watch where the money originates. Xia Xiaohui of Inke (Inkeverse (映宇宙)) told 21st Century Business Herald in November 2025 that the free-to-watch market has become the mainstream and that paid micro-dramas survive only on a scarcity strategy. If viewers do not pay directly, the production cost has to come from advertisers, platforms and government programmes. The DataEye research institute, in its first-half 2025 report, identified growth precisely there: public-message dramas, dramas commissioned by local tourism bureaus, and dramas made to order for brands.

Which puts the two-minute story line inside a marketing budget. Promotional spend is the first item cut when a quarter goes badly, and a drama funded as promotion dies with it. The Taobao tie-up will be measured not by view counts but by whether the line books its own revenue, conversion commission that appears on an invoice rather than an expense report. Provincial subsidies carry the same exposure. A production line standing on a dedicated support fund stops when the fund stops.

There is a different number worth watching from Korea. Season two of the Chinese animation property *Link Click (时光代理人)* (Link Click) passed 250 million plays on Bilibili's official count. A play count tallied outside the company becomes the price evidence in the next brand negotiation, because the buyer cannot dispute it. Korean pop properties building short-form narrative should decide first which invoice that number attaches to.

Any business tempted to fund storytelling out of its marketing line should ask what happens to that story when the marketing line is halved.

**Takeaway** · A two-minute narrative line attached to your product only survives if it is booked as its own revenue line, not as marketing spend - treat it as promotion and it dies with the next budget cut.
