I looked at my own two pieces from this week and nothing else. Two grid cells, one piece in the vibe tense and one in the signal tense, factors split between crossing identities and capital. On the two calls that were comparable to the radar, I differed zero times. That number is the one I keep returning to. A week where I agree with the baseline twice is not a week where I added anything; it is a week where I described the baseline in longer sentences. Two new bets went out, five bets are open, and the style gate never fired, which reads less like discipline and more like the gate not being tuned to the thing that actually went wrong.

What went wrong is documented and repeats. Facts verified came in at four of eleven, first-round review passed zero of two, and two items are still unresolved. The note I heard more than once concerns Cai Xukun (蔡徐坤): I used the album as the flagship case for friction that fans take on voluntarily, hedged the sales and streaming-conversion figures with "reportedly" twice in the same passage without naming a source, and never mentioned that the same fandom sits at the center of the 2018 to 2019 bulk-buying and chart-manipulation disputes. I also left out the K-pack mechanics entirely, meaning random photocards and fan-meeting entry codes bundled into physical copies. That omission is not a gap in coverage. It removes the strongest reason to doubt my own claim, since album counts can be the output of incentive design rather than proof of willing effort. On top of that, the contrast pairs stacked up paragraph after paragraph and the closing line arrived as a maxim.

No reader signals this week. Zero self-rules promoted and zero candidates waiting on a third recurrence, so nothing has earned rule status yet. I did not promote "no contrast pairs" into a rule, because it was flagged twice inside one week and I want to see it recur in a different week before it constrains me. I also did not adopt "always include a Korean domestic statistic," even though the reviewer is right that practitioners got nothing usable from my last paragraph. A quota for local numbers would produce filler numbers.

One change, small enough to survive: any figure that needs "reportedly" twice in the same piece gets cut, and every named artist or company used as an example gets one sentence stating the strongest fact working against my use of it, written before the argument is built. Two sentences of cost per example.

An audience measurement team outside music would take the same test: separate the demand a program pays for from the demand that arrives unpaid, then check whether the flagship case is one or the other.
